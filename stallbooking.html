6 Rules, Regulations & Declaration

  1. Eligibility & Application: Participation is subject to acceptance of the completed application by TNDTMA and availability of exhibition space. Submission of this form does not by itself constitute confirmation of allotment.
  2. Stall Allotment: TNDTMA reserves the right to determine stall location, size and category based on the approved floor plan, operational requirements and availability. Stall allotment will be confirmed separately.
  3. Payment: The exhibitor shall make payments according to the schedule communicated by TNDTMA. Taxes, statutory charges and additional services, where applicable, shall be payable by the exhibitor.
  4. Confirmation & Cancellation: Stall confirmation, cancellation and refund conditions shall be governed by the terms communicated in the official exhibitor agreement / allotment letter. TNDTMA reserves the right to reallocate space in case of non-payment within the stipulated period.
  5. Sub-Letting / Sharing: Sub-letting, assignment or sharing of the allotted stall with another company or organisation is not permitted without prior written approval from TNDTMA.
  6. Exhibitor Products & Activities: Exhibitors shall display and promote only lawful products, services and educational activities relevant to the exhibition. Any activity that may cause nuisance, obstruction or safety concerns is prohibited.
  7. Branding & Stall Construction: All stall construction, branding, electrical work, demonstrations and special installations must comply with technical specifications, height limits and deadlines communicated by TNDTMA and the appointed exhibition contractor.
  8. Move-in & Move-out: Exhibitors shall move-in, set up and remove exhibits strictly within the timings communicated by TNDTMA. Goods, displays and materials must be removed promptly after the exhibition closes.
  9. Safety & Compliance: Exhibitors are responsible for the safe operation of equipment, demonstrations and materials within their stall and shall comply with venue, fire-safety, electrical and other applicable regulations.
  10. Conduct: Exhibitors, their representatives, contractors and staff shall maintain professional conduct and shall not obstruct aisles, neighbouring stalls, entrances, exits or common areas.
  11. Photography / Publicity: TNDTMA may photograph or record the exhibition and use such material for legitimate event documentation and promotional purposes. Exhibitors shall not make public statements representing the exhibition or TNDTMA without prior approval.
  12. Confidentiality: Exhibitors shall not disclose or misuse confidential business information belonging to TNDTMA, other exhibitors, speakers, visitors or partners that they may access during the event.
  13. Liability: TNDTMA shall not be liable for loss, theft, damage, injury or interruption except to the extent required by applicable law. Exhibitors are responsible for securing their own exhibits, materials and insurance.
  14. Termination of Right to Exhibit: TNDTMA may terminate an exhibitor’s participation for breach of these rules, non-payment, unlawful activity, safety concerns, misrepresentation or conduct that materially affects the exhibition.
  15. Cancellation / Changes to Exhibition: TNDTMA reserves the right, where reasonably necessary, to modify the floor plan, venue arrangements, timings or other operational aspects of the exhibition.
  16. Governing Law: These Rules & Regulations shall be governed by the laws applicable in India.
  17. Dispute Resolution: Any dispute relating to participation shall first be addressed through good-faith discussion with TNDTMA.
